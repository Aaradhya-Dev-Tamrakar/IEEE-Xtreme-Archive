# Sugarel and Modulo

**Time Limit:** `1000 ms`  
**Memory Limit:** `256 MB`  
**Source:** [https://csacademy.com/contest/archive/task/Sugarel-and-modulo/](https://csacademy.com/contest/archive/task/Sugarel-and-modulo/)  

---

Today Sugarel discovered modulo operation. To understand better how this operation modifies the numbers, he takes an interval of integer numbers $[A, B]$ and applies $\text{modulo}$ operation with a number $M$ for each of them. Now he is asking what is the largest result.

### Standard input

The first line contains three integers $A$, $B$ and $M$.

### Standard output

Output a single number, the largest number from the interval $[A, B]$ after Sugerel applies the $\text{modulo}$ operation.

### Constraints and notes

$1 \leq A \leq B \leq 10^{18}$ $1 \leq M \leq 10^{18}$ 

| Input | Output |
| --- | --- |
| 5 19 8 | 7 |
| 3 6 10 | 6 |

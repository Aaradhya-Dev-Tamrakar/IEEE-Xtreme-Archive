# Bottle Recycling

**Time Limit:** `1000 ms`  
**Memory Limit:** `128 MB`  
**Source:** [https://csacademy.com/contest/archive/task/bottle-recycling/](https://csacademy.com/contest/archive/task/bottle-recycling/)  

---

You have $N$ bottles of juice. At the store next street there is a promotion: for $K$ empty bottles, they give you another full one! You are wondering what's the maximum number of bottles you can drink by taking full advantage of this promotion.

### Standard input

The first line contains two integers $N$ and $K$.

### Standard output

Print a single integer, the total number of bottles you can drink.

### Constraints and notes

$1 \leq N \leq 10^9$ $2 \leq K \leq 10^9$

| Input | Output | Explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9 3 | 13 | You drink the $9$ bottles, and the store gives you $3$ more for the empty bottles. After drinking these $3$, the store gives you one more for their bottles. Thus, the total number of bottles is $9+3+1$. View |
| 11 4 | 14 | View |

# Greatest Common Divisor

**Time Limit:** `500 ms`  
**Memory Limit:** `128 MB`  
**Source:** [https://csacademy.com/contest/archive/task/gcd/](https://csacademy.com/contest/archive/task/gcd/)  

---

We say that an integer $D$ is a divisor of another integer $A$ if the fraction $A/D$ is also an integer. Given two positive integers $A$ and $B$, compute the largest number which is a divisor of both $A$ and $B$.

### Standard input

The first line contains the two integers $A$ and $B$.

### Standard output

Ouput a single number representing the greatest common divisor.

### Constraints and notes

$1 \leq A, B \leq 10^9$

| Input | Output |
| --- | --- |
| 10 20 | 10 |
| 6 10 | 2 |

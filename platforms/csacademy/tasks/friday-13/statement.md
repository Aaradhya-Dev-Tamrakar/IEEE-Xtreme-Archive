# Friday 13

**Time Limit:** `1000 ms`  
**Memory Limit:** `256 MB`  
**Source:** [https://csacademy.com/contest/archive/task/friday-13/](https://csacademy.com/contest/archive/task/friday-13/)  

---

In this problem you should count how many times the $13^{th}$ day of the month will be on a Friday throughout a year.

We consider a year consists of $12$ months and  $365$ days. The number of days of each month is represented by the array $[31, 28, 31, 30, 31, 30, 31, 31, 30, 31, 30, 31]$.

### Standard input

The first line contains one of the following strings: "Monday", "Tuesday", "Wednesday", "Thursday", "Friday", "Saturday", "Sunday", representing the day of the week of January $1^{st}$.

### Standard output

Print the answer on the first line.

| Input | Output | Explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sunday | 2 | $1^{st}$ of Jan 2017 was a Sunday and 2017 has 365 days.In January and October $13^{th}$ will be on Friday. |

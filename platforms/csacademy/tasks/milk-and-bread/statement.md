# Milk and Bread

**Time Limit:** `1000 ms`  
**Memory Limit:** `256 MB`  
**Source:** [https://csacademy.com/contest/archive/task/milk-and-bread/](https://csacademy.com/contest/archive/task/milk-and-bread/)  

---

You have a new task: buy milk and bread.

You are given three integers representing the coordinates of the milk shop, the bread shop, and yours on the x-axis. Find the minimum distance you need to travel to visit both the milk shop and the bread shop, in any order.

### Standard input

The first line contains three integers $x_1$, $x_2$ and $x_3$, representing the coordinates of the milk shop, the bread shop, and yours, respectively.

### Standard output

Print the answer on the first line.

### Constraints and notes

$0 \leq x_1, x_2, x_3 \leq 1000$ The three integers are pairwise distinct

| Input | Output |
| --- | --- |
| 1 10 2 | 10 |
| 1 5 10 | 9 |

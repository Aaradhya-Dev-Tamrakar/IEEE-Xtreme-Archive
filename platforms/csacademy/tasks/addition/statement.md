# Addition

**Time Limit:** `500 ms`  
**Memory Limit:** `128 MB`  
**Source:** [https://csacademy.com/contest/archive/task/addition/](https://csacademy.com/contest/archive/task/addition/)  

---

You are given two integer values $A$ and $B$, compute and output their sum.

### Standard input

The first line contains the two integers $A$ and $B$.

### Standard output

Output a single number representing the sum of $A$ and $B$.

### Constraints and notes

$-10^9 \leq A, B \leq 10^9$

| Input | Output |
| --- | --- |
| 10 20 | 30 |
| 333 876 | 1209 |
